## Three-stage care report

All three frames use the same fictional modern Armenian grave in a fixed 3:2 smartphone-report composition. The plot contains one blank upright charcoal-granite memorial, a flat dark-gray granite cover slab and a low rectangular border of dark Armenian basalt. The close crop includes a worn concrete path, a warm volcanic-stone retaining wall and a small cypress branch, but no other graves. The memorial always remains blank: no names, dates, portraits, engravings, crosses, symbols or identifying details.

### Before

Create a natural late-morning documentary photograph of the single plot after a dry season without care. Add clearly visible summer dust and windblown soil across the slab and horizontal stone, scattered dry leaves, brittle stems, and uneven short-to-knee-high dry grass growing densely in the soil channels and border joints. Retain one faded bouquet. Keep the grave fully visible and structurally intact. The picture should resemble a believable, slightly imperfect smartphone status photo rather than premium memorial architecture or a cemetery panorama.

### During

Use precise image editing on the generated before frame. Preserve the camera, lighting, wall, path and every stone joint. Show no person or body part. Make the process visible through a used dark-green water bucket, stiff brush, damp gray cloth, pruning shears, hand rake and an open refuse bag containing weeds and the dried bouquet. Leave some grass uncut, a loose pile of pulled weeds, an irregular cleaned swath on the dusty slab, and believable wet patches and muddy drips. Avoid staged tools, machinery, new construction or magical restoration.

### After

Use precise image editing on the clean after frame. Preserve the exact structure, camera and weathered material character. Remove weeds, leaves, dust and tools; sweep the old concrete and wash the existing stone without making it showroom-new. Place one medium, low-profile hand-tied arrangement of cream-white roses, blush carnations, small white chrysanthemums and restrained green foliage diagonally on the upper third of the cover slab. Use plain twine and no ribbon or text. Change only the maintenance condition and flowers—never the architecture, paving, planting or grave design.
